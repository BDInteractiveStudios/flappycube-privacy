Data Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the date and time of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent using the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

If you create an account, the Application also collects:

  • Your username and password (stored securely via Unity Authentication)
  • Game data such as high scores, coins, statistics, and unlocked items (stored via Unity Cloud Save)
  • Your Google Play Games profile information if you sign in with Google

The Application does not collect precise location information from your mobile device.

The Application does not use artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or to provide features.

The collected data is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the game functionality, including cloud saves and leaderboards
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To improve the Application and user experience

Legal Basis for Processing (Art. 6 GDPR)

The processing of your personal data is based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Processing necessary to provide game functionality, including account management, cloud saves, and leaderboards.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Personalized advertising through Google AdMob is only enabled after you have given your consent via the consent dialog (for users in the EEA).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Non-personalized advertising and general improvement of the Application. Our legitimate interest is to finance the free Application through advertising and to improve the user experience.
