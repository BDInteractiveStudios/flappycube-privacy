Delete Account – Flappy Cube

Here you can request the deletion of your Flappy Cube account and all associated data.

What will be deleted:
  • Your username and password
  • Your high score and all game statistics
  • Your coins and unlocked colors
  • All data stored in the cloud
  • Your leaderboard entries

Deletion will be completed within 30 days of your request. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

Via email:
  1. Send an email to our support team
  2. Subject: "Delete Account – Flappy Cube"
  3. Include your Player ID (you can find it in the app under Statistics)
  4. You will receive a confirmation within 7 days
